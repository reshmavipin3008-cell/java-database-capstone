# User Story: View Appointment Calendar

**Title:**
_As a doctor, I want to view my appointment calendar, so that I can stay organized throughout the day and week._

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Visual calendar component supporting daily, weekly, and monthly views.
2. Displays booked appointments with time slot, patient name, and visit status.
3. Clicking on an entry opens quick details for that specific appointment slot.

**Priority:** High
**Story Points:** 5
**Notes:**
- Highlight current time and upcoming consultations dynamically.
